Write-up

Blotter

Only trades that actually happened: every entry and exit, in order. No working orders, no signals. Cash delta is 100 × price for options and stock, and only these rows move cash.

Week by week

One row per expiry Friday: what strike the rule picked, what the market looked like when I wrote it, and how the week ended. Skipped weeks say why.

    Ledger

    The book at each timestamp: shares, the short call (strike and expiry), cash, and marks. Stock is marked at the bar's stock print, the call at its mid (last mid if a bar has no quote, flagged stale). The short call counts as negative.

    Rows

    Reg T account

    Reg T, not portfolio margin. I can work every number out from the ledger without a broker's risk engine.

    NAV
    cash + stock MV + option MV (short call is negative)
    LMV
    shares × stock mark
    Initial
    50% of stock LMV (a covered short call adds $0)
    Maintenance
    25% of stock LMV (FINRA)
    Available funds
    NAV − Initial
    Excess
    NAV − Maintenance
