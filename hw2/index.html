Write-up

PMCC blotter

Every trade that actually happened on the diagonal: the one long call purchase, and each week's short call sold, expired, or bought back. No working orders, no signals.

PMCC week by week

One row per expiry Friday: the short strike the rule picked, the premium, and how the week ended. "Closed" means I bought the short call back at its own mark rather than let it run into an assignment I can't cover.

    PMCC account

    Cash, the long call's mark, the short call's mark (if one is open), and NAV at each timestamp. The "capital required" line is explained in the write-up.

    Rows

    PMCC vs covered call vs buy & hold

    Same stock, same weekly short-call rule (3% OTM, filled at mid). The only thing that changes is what collateralizes the short call: 100 shares (covered call), one deep-in-the-money call (PMCC), or nothing - just holding the stock.

    NAV, each on its own capital

    Every series starts at 100% of its own starting cash, so this reads as return on capital deployed, not dollars - PMCC is scaled up far more by the same dollar move in DAL.

    Covered call (comparison baseline)

    Same engine as HW1.2: buy 100 shares, sell a weekly call at 3% OTM, wait through expiry, Reg T margin. Shown here only as the capital-heavy baseline PMCC is compared against.

      Blotter

      Mid vs trade

      Every bar where a short call both had a real bid/ask and a trade printed (the same weekly chain backs both strategies' short leg). If trades sit on the line, mid is a fair price to assume for my fills. Y is TRDPRC_1, X is (BID+ASK)/2, same bar.

      Checks

      Re-derived independently from the raw bars, not from the engine's own bookkeeping. If one fails it shows here.
